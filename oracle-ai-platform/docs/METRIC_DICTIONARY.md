# Metric and rule dictionary

Generated by `npm run docs` from the product modules. Numbers are calculated by deterministic code with exact decimals; the AI never produces them. Portfolio rates aggregate numerators and denominators; they are never averages of percentages.

### Assessment rules

| Rule | Severity | Automation | Owner | Description | Measurement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `bounce_rate-v1` | high | B | Email operations | Campaign bounce rate (hard + soft bouncebacks / total sends) exceeds the approved threshold for campaigns above the minimum send volume. | Re-run after the next send; the rule clears when bounce rate is at or below the threshold. |
| `unsubscribe_rate-v1` | medium | B | Campaign manager | Unsubscribes / delivered exceeds the approved threshold for a campaign above minimum volume. | Track unsubscribe rate on the next comparable send. |
| `ctor_gap-v1` | low | B | Content owner | Unique click-to-open rate is below the portfolio rate by at least the approved gap, for a campaign above minimum volume. | Compare click-to-open on the next send against the portfolio baseline. |
| `immature_activity-v1` | info | A | Analyst | Sends within the activity look-back window of the data's as-of time can still receive opens, clicks and form submissions. | Rates for these campaigns stabilize after the look-back window. |
| `unmatched_activity-v1` | medium | B | Marketing operations | Activities reference campaigns or contacts not present in this snapshot, or opens/clicks have no matching send in scope. | Rule clears when unmatched activities fall to zero for the same scope. |
| `exposure-v1` | medium | B | Campaign manager | Contacts received more emails than the approved maximum within the exposure window. This is a rule-based flag, separate from Oracle's native fatigue analysis. | Count of contacts above the threshold in the next snapshot. |
| `nurture_unreachable-v1` | medium | B | Campaign builder | A canvas step has no path from any entry step in the supplied structure. | Rule clears when the step is reachable in the next structure export. |
| `nurture_dead_end-v1` | low | B | Campaign builder | A non-end step has no next step in the supplied structure. | Rule clears when the path has an explicit continuation or end. |
| `nurture_drop-v1` | medium | B | Campaign manager | Among contacts who entered a step (above minimum cohort), the share later entering a connected next step is below 100% − the approved drop-off threshold. | Progression share for this step in the next step-history export. |
| `asset_readiness-v1` | low | B | Content owner | An email asset fails a metadata, HTML or personalization check. A delivered-HTML check is not proof of inbox rendering or link availability. | Rule clears when the asset passes all checks in the next export. |
| `click_to_form-v1` | low | B | Web / form owner | Among eligible clickers (click at least one attribution window before as-of), the share with a linked form submission within the window is below 100% − the approved drop-off threshold. | Click-to-form progression in the next activity export. |
| `stalled_mqls-v1` | high | B | Sales development lead | Leads reached MQL but have no sales-accepted, SQL, opportunity or disqualified date after the approved number of days. | Count of MQLs waiting longer than the threshold in the next lead export. |
| `low_mql_acceptance-v1` | medium | B | Marketing operations with sales leadership | Among mature MQLs (older than the lead maturity window), the share that reached sales-accepted is below the approved threshold. | MQL-to-SAL share for the next mature cohort of the same sources. |
| `aging_pipeline-v1` | medium | B | Sales operations | Open opportunities were created more than the approved number of days before the as-of time. | Value and count of aged open opportunities in the next export. |
| `campaign_no_pipeline-v1` | medium | B | Campaign manager | A campaign recorded at least the minimum cohort of mature leads, and none of them has an opportunity date or a linked opportunity. | Opportunities from the campaign's next mature lead cohort. |
| `event_low_attendance-v1` | medium | B | Event marketing | A past event with at least the minimum registrations had an attendance rate (attended / non-cancelled registrations) below the threshold. | Attendance rate of the next comparable event. |
| `website_low_known-v1` | low | B | Web / marketing operations | The share of website visits linked to a known Eloqua contact is below the approved threshold. | Known-visitor share in the next visit export. |
| `landing_short_visits-v1` | low | B | Content / web owner | A landing page with at least the minimum timed visits has a median visit duration below the approved number of seconds. | Median visit duration for this landing page in the next export. |

### Scenario inputs

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Rule | Severity | Automation | Owner | Description | Measurement |

| `missing_required_field-v1` | medium | B | Data steward | Source records are missing a field the customer marked as required for this purpose. | Field completeness in the next profile snapshot. |

|---|---|---|
| minSends | 100 | Eloqua campaign rules apply only above this volume |
| bounceRateThreshold | 5 % | Eloqua bounce rule |
| unsubscribeRateThreshold | 0.5 % | Eloqua unsubscribe rule |
| ctorGapPp | 5 pp | Eloqua click-to-open gap vs portfolio |

| attributionWindowDays | 7 | Eloqua click-to-form window |
| exposureMaxSends / exposureWindowDays | none (must be approved) | Eloqua exposure flag |
| deliveryFromBounces | false | Derive delivered = sends − bounces only when approved |

| dropThreshold | 30 % | Eloqua nurture / click-to-form, Infinity journey drop-off |
| requiredFields | [] (must be chosen) | Unity completeness |
| staleDays | 30 | Unity record freshness; report staleness banner |
| snapshotToleranceHours | 1 | Unity exact overlap compatibility |
| conversionEvent | none (must be named) | Infinity event conversion |
| funnelSteps / funnelWindowMinutes / funnelIdentity | none / 30 / session | Infinity journey |
| lateMinutes | 60 | Infinity late events |
| minHistoryDays / anomalyZ | 14 / 3 | Infinity volume anomaly |
